Our Policy

Please read before you book — we need to make sure we’re all on the same page.

Disclaimer

Naseeha Sessions provides faith-based Islamic guidance concerning mental, emotional, spiritual, and personal difficulties. Our services are religious in nature and do not constitute clinical mental health treatment, psychotherapy, diagnosis, or medical care.

We are not clinical psychologists or psychiatrists; we offer naseeha (Islamic advisory) and personal development guidance.

No Diagnosis or Clinical Treatment

Naseeha Sessions does not diagnose mental health conditions, conduct clinical assessments, prescribe medication, or provide clinical treatment. Our role is limited to offering faith-based Islamic advice and guidance rooted in the Qur’an and Sunnah.

If we cannot help your case, we will refer you to a licensed mental health professional in your area.

🚫 No Legal or Court Involvement Policy

Naseeha Sessions with Rahil is not involved in any legal matters, and we do not participate in any court-related proceedings under any circumstances.

Our role is strictly limited to naseeha (Islamic advisory) and personal development. We are not legal professionals, and we do not offer legal advice or services. To be clear:

  • We do not provide written or verbal statements, reports, summaries, or documents for legal use.
  • We do not assist with court filings, custody cases, divorce documents, or any personal or professional legal claims.
  • We do not make legal appearances, submit testimony, or communicate with legal representatives on your behalf.
  • We do not give any information (written, verbal, or recorded) to support lawsuits or court proceedings.
  • Any attempt to involve us in legal disputes will be refused and may result in immediate termination of naseeha services.
  • If you require legal support, you must consult a qualified attorney.
  • We are not to be contacted by legal authorities, lawyers, or courts for any purpose related to our naseeha services.

By using the services of Naseeha Sessions, you acknowledge and agree to this policy in full.

Code of Conduct for Clients

  • Be on time.
  • We recommend you keep your video off if you are meeting with an advisor of the opposite gender.
  • Respect the boundaries being put in place by the advisor.
  • Your advisor is not responsible for filling a void; rather their objective is to be empathetic, help you sort out your issues, and propose solutions.
  • Do not discuss or share personal matters that are irrelevant to your case.
  • The advisor has the right to discontinue or decline sessions and refer you to another advisor.
  • Your well-being is important to us. How To Book A Session — Session Guidelines

Session length: Each session lasts one hour, depending on what you book. Be mindful of time so we can conclude the session smoothly.

Respect our time: Sessions take place in Google Meet. Tech glitches happen, but do your best to be there at the start.

No-Show Policy

If you do not join within the first 10 minutes of your session, the session is over. It will be considered a no-show/cancellation, and for paid sessions no refund will be provided. This applies to all sessions. No exceptions.

Free Naseeha Offer

We offer free naseeha for those who truly can’t afford it. But let’s keep it real — this is a trust. Please respect our time and don’t abuse this offer. If you can afford paid sessions, please go for that option. For those who need the free offer, we ask that you follow the guidelines below:

  • If you do not show up within the first 10 minutes, the session will be canceled and you will not be able to book for another month — no exceptions.
  • Limited bookings: You can book up to 2 free sessions per month unless your advisor approves additional sessions. This helps ensure that everyone gets a fair chance to access our services.
  • Abuse of service: If we find that you’re abusing the free option, we reserve the right to discontinue your access to it.
  • Gender-specific sessions: Brothers should book with the male advisor only. The sister does not offer free sessions to brothers.

Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

If you need to cancel or reschedule your session, please do so at least 3 days before the scheduled session. You can find the cancellation or rescheduling link in your confirmation email or within your calendar event notes.

No cancellations or rescheduling after the 3-day window.

If you do not show up within the first 10 minutes of your session, the session will be over and automatically canceled. No refunds will be provided for attending late past the 10-minute mark. No exceptions. Please respect our time!
